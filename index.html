About

Hi there! I am a doctoral student at UNC Chapel Hill and the Graduate Director of Digital Humanities at the ECL’s Digital Literacy and Communications Lab. His research interrogates how memes produce rhetorical structures of experience that affect the formation of collective belonging and identity as they circulate within, between, and beyond digital publics.
